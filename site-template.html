About me

Helping people navigate the details that matter.

I study business administration at Michigan Ross and work at the University of Michigan Student Organization Account Service. My work brings together financial account support, data accuracy, service, and clear communication.

Since August 2026, I have helped student leaders understand university financial guidelines and navigate account needs for a service office that supports more than 1,700 student organizations. I use the SOAS Dashboard and Google Suite to maintain accounts and help with adjustments, payment requests, freezes, deficits, and signer changes. I respond to questions by email, phone, and in person.

At Visit Detroit, where I interned from May through August 2026, I supported the partner lifecycle: collecting information, updating iDSS records, building directory listings, creating account access, monitoring communication, and following up with partners. I also contributed to event operations and a restaurant-partner data review that corrected outdated records and clarified partner categories.

Earlier, I supported community projects through General Motors and helped students build confidence in mathematics through Wayne State University’s Math Corps. Across each role, I have learned how strong operations, thoughtful communication, and accurate information create better experiences for people.
