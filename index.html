About me

A versatile emerging business professional.

My experience does not fit into only one box—and that is one of my strengths. I have worked with data, partners, events, community initiatives, students, and cross-functional teams.

At Visit Detroit, I support the full partner lifecycle: collecting submitted information, updating iDSS records, building directory listings, creating account access, explaining digital tools, connecting contacts to newsletters and distribution lists, monitoring engagement, and following up when communication is not reaching the right people.

I also contribute to event operations and special projects, including partner mixers, Hometown Tourist Day, the Detroit Grand Prix, National Celebrate Services Week, a tourism analytics education session, and the National Veterans Wheelchair Games. My most significant data-quality project focused on reviewing restaurant partner records, correcting outdated information, and improving how paid partners and Neighborhood Promotion Program participants are categorized.

Earlier, I supported community projects through General Motors and helped students build confidence in mathematics through Wayne State University’s Math Corps. Across each role, I have learned how strong operations, thoughtful communication, and accurate information create better experiences for people.
